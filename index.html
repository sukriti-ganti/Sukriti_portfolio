About

Who am I?

An ECE undergrad specializing in 5G/6G Wireless Technologies, bridging theory and silicon through hands-on FPGA engineering. At Microchip Technology's FPGA Business Unit I've implemented an 81-tap folded systolic FIR filter for 5G NR O-RAN channelization on PolarFire, and I'm currently building a fully parametric Generic FIR IP Block — any tap count, any clock/data rate, static or runtime-reloadable coefficients.

  • 📡 Wireless & O-RAN: propagation, fading, cellular architecture, 5G/NR
  • 🔧 FPGA Engineering: Libero SoC, Synplify, SmartTime, AXI4/AXI4-Stream
  • 🧮 DSP & Filtering: symmetric folding, polyphase decomposition, FFT, PSD
  • 💻 Software: Python (bit-true models), MATLAB, Verilog/VHDL RTL
